Publisher / homepage:

Muse Auto-Allow has one job: on **muse.ai** pages, it automatically clicks the

**Allow** button on Muse's approval requests so your Muse agent does not stop for

manual approvals. This policy explains exactly what that involves — which is very little,

because the extension keeps everything on your own device.

---

1. Summary (the short version)

  • The extension reads the content of **muse.ai** pages only, and only to find Muse

approval buttons.

  • It stores your settings (on/off, click delay, click counter), a local log of the **last 50 clicks** (each with a timestamp), and **compact click statistics** (per day / month / year and per topic) in your browser.
  • It **collects nothing** about you.
  • It **transmits nothing** to us or anyone else.
  • There is **no analytics**, **no tracking**, and **no advertising**.
  • There is **no remote code** — all code is bundled inside the package.
  • The optional **settings sync** (off by default) mirrors only your settings through your own Google Account; logs and statistics are never synced.

---

2. What the extension can access, and why

**Page content of muse.ai tabs.** To find and click the correct button, the extension's

content script reads the page structure (DOM) of Muse pages you have open. This access is

limited to `https://muse.ai/*` and `https://*.muse.ai/*`. The extension requests no access

to any other website.

**Your browser storage.** The extension saves, locally in your browser

(`chrome.storage.local`):

  • your settings — whether auto-clicking is on or off, the delay between clicks, dry-run and allow-once preferences, topic and keyword rules, your follow-up options (prompts, chips, smart follow-up, schedule), a mission choice, and a click counter;
  • a click log — the **last 50 actions** it performed, each described by timestamp, kind

(allow / expand / open panel / review bar / blocked / follow-up / mission), button label,

and context;

  • click statistics — compact counters only: clicks per day, per month, per year, and per topic

(a repository or host name, up to 40 characters, taken from the approval text). No page

content is stored.

This information never leaves your device — with one exception you control: the optional

**settings sync** described below.

**Optional settings sync (off by default).** If you turn on **Sync settings** in the

extension, a compact settings-only subset — on/off state, click delay, dry-run and

allow-once preferences, topic and keyword rules, and your follow-up options (prompts,

chips, smart follow-up, and schedule) — is mirrored through `chrome.storage.sync` into

your own Google Account, so your settings follow you to your other Chrome profiles. Your

click log, click statistics, counters and digest timestamps are **never** synced. You can

turn it off at any time; removing the synced copy uses Chrome's own sync data controls.

**Desktop notifications (the digest).** On browser startup, the extension can show one

desktop notification summarizing recent local activity (clicks, follow-ups, blocked

items). This summary is composed on your device from your own local counters; the

`notifications` permission is used only to display it. No data leaves your device.

**A once-a-minute timer.** The extension uses a one-minute alarm purely to check for

approval requests in background tabs. It makes no network requests.

---

3. What the extension does NOT do

  • It does **not** collect personal data of any kind (no names, emails, identifiers,

location, health, financial, or authentication data).

  • It does **not** read, store, or send the content of any page other than the muse.ai

button detection described above.

  • It does **not** collect your web history, keystrokes, or activity outside muse.ai.
  • It does **not** transmit any data to our servers or to any third party — **there are no

servers**. The extension makes no outbound network calls (the optional settings sync uses

Chrome's built-in sync into your own account, never a server of ours).

  • It does **not** sell, rent, or share data, because it holds no data to sell.
  • It does **not** use data for advertising, profiling, creditworthiness, or lending.
  • It does **not** load or execute remote code. All JavaScript ships inside the extension

package; nothing is downloaded or evaluated at runtime.

---

4. Data storage, retention, and deletion

Everything the extension keeps is stored locally in your browser and stays under your

control:

  • **Retention:** the click log keeps only the most recent 50 entries; older entries are

discarded automatically.

  • **Retention (statistics):** the statistics counters keep at most 400 days, 24 months and

100 topics; older entries are discarded automatically.

  • **Deletion:** remove the extension from Chrome (`chrome://extensions` → Remove) to

delete all stored settings and logs. You can also clear the extension's data from your

browser's site/extension data controls.

If you enabled the optional settings sync, turning it off stops future mirroring; deleting the

synced copy uses your Google Account's sync data controls.

Because we never receive this data, there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

---

5. Permissions explained

| Permission | Why it is needed |

|---|---|

| `storage` | Save your on/off setting, click delay, click counter, the local last-50 click log, and compact click statistics. |

| `alarms` | Run a once-a-minute check so approvals in background tabs are handled. |

| `notifications` | Show the optional startup digest. It is composed locally on your device; no data leaves it. |

| Host: `https://muse.ai/*`, `https://*.muse.ai/*` | Detect and click the Allow button on Muse pages. No other site is accessed. |

The extension deliberately does **not** request `tabs`, `scripting`, `webRequest`,

`cookies`, `downloads`, or `<all_urls>`. Its two keyboard shortcuts (`commands`: pause,

follow-up) are a manifest key, not a permission.
